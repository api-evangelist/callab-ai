Callab AI, built by Clusterlabs, is a cloud-based voice AI platform that connects directly to legacy on-premises PBX/SBC telephony (Cisco UCM, Mitel, FreeSWITCH) via SIP trunk integration, routing calls to AI voice agents with no rip-and-replace migration. The platform automates up to 70% of inbound and outbound call volume across support, sales, and scheduling, with no-code agent building, live human supervision and takeover, semantic knowledge bases, post-call transcription and sentiment analysis, CRM data extraction, and batch outbound campaigns. Callab AI exposes a REST API at docs.callab.ai for programmatic control of agents and calls. A Y Combinator Spring 2026 company founded in 2024, it serves hospitality, logistics, retail, and property management across three continents.

**Areas:** Voice AI · Conversational AI · Telephony · Contact Center · SIP · Voice Agents · Call Automation · PBX · Customer Service · AI Agents

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Callab AI: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
